# ADR 0047: Sprint 11 Closeout and Diagnostic Method Refinement

## Status

Accepted as the Patch 061 Sprint 11 closeout design. A fresh unmodified
all-tools campaign remains an empirical acceptance requirement.

## Context

Sprint 11 established a high-resolution runner, a reproducible provisional
corpus, task-normalized baseline adapters, matched x64lens relations, runtime-
closure evidence, address-coordinate calibration, a 30-condition campaign
authority, generated summaries, and an engineering gap register.

The retained cloud checkpoint accounted for 30 planned conditions while
executing the 12 available x64lens conditions and retaining 18 pinned-baseline
conditions as unavailable. A later, separately qualified WSL2 replay used the
exact pinned baselines and, after one narrow evidence-local correction to the
ROPgadget 7.7 banner authority, executed all 30 conditions and retained 180
successful process rows plus 24 normalized relations. Two Python task-path
closures and coordinate calibration failed, so the replay was not
comparison-qualified and did not replace the fresh unmodified Patch 061
campaign.

The WSL2 replay was useful because it exposed unresolved method boundaries:

- all 12 x64lens single-process conditions were below the measured 6,361,100 ns
  reliable floor;
- tool-native record totals represented different populations;
- the first normalized exact relation supplied no positive coordinate anchor;
- Python isolated-environment context was lost in the initial closure path;
- native execution success could be confused with comparison qualification;
- several transaction paths did not retain pathname or object identity strongly
  enough through cleanup and publication.

## Decision

Patch 061 closes Sprint 11 after correcting the measurement plane and preserving
its limitations as evidence.

The patch:

1. separates native execution status from normalized comparison qualification;
2. makes runtime-closure and coordinate state independently visible;
3. rejects recursive generic gadget-count authority;
4. binds all imported generator code in the campaign manifest;
5. preserves Python isolated-environment launcher and prefix evidence;
6. removes retained staging-path lineage from published relation and closure
   artifacts;
7. rejects symlinked input/output ancestors and strengthens owned-object cleanup;
8. records a preregistered below-floor whole-batch policy without inventing
   divided single-run latency;
9. records the diagnostic assessment and closes Sprint 11 without promoting its
   rows to release evidence.

## Benchmark interpretation

A below-floor successful process is an unresolved single-run measurement, not a
zero-duration run. A baseline-native record count remains tool-specific until a
named relation and reconciliation rule exist. Empty intersections cannot
qualify address coordinates.

The initial selected exact relation is retained as a valid negative observation,
but it is not sufficient for broad coverage comparison. Future diagnostic
relations must be selected because they support a named defensive or research
task and must preserve native output and duplicate policy.

## Roadmap consequence

Sprint 12 begins with this order:

1. program-header validity and explicit extended-numbering outcomes;
2. overlapping executable-region and provenance semantics;
3. PIE executable versus shared-object identity;
4. bounded GNU-property IBT and SHSTK evidence;
5. a corrected held-out diagnostic rerun with positive coordinate anchors and
   five complete task-path closures;
6. measurement refinements such as below-floor batching, RSS calibration,
   phase attribution, and bounded reporter buffering.

Candidate-scoped decoding and concurrency remain optional Sprint 14 profiles.
Neither becomes mandatory from generic count disagreement or unresolved timing.

## Consequences

- The reference analyzer remains dependency-free, decoder-free, one-worker,
  bounded, deterministic, and independently measurable.
- No analyzer assembly, CLI, schema, record layout, score, capacity, or arena
  change is introduced by Patch 061.
- Sprint 11 is a completed diagnostic checkpoint, not a confirmatory campaign.
- A fresh all-tools campaign under the corrected authority remains an empirical
  acceptance requirement.
- Every retained Sprint 11 row and artifact remains diagnostic, unfrozen, and
  publication-ineligible.
